Free and subscriptions

What's free, how PRO and PRO+ differ, restoring, and canceling.

    What's free

    Logging workouts is free with no limit. You can make up to 3 routines and 5 custom exercises.

    The 1,690 exercises and how-to guides, estimated weights for 3, 5, 8, and 10 reps, the plate calculator, the recovery map, sync across devices, backup files, health apps, watches, sending to a trainer, and the today's workout widget are free too. You can keep 1 client and 1 equipment location.

    PRO

    PRO works out what to lift next.

    • Routine auto progression, a personal 1–15 rep curve, and in-workout next-weight and stop suggestions
    • Effort calibration learned from your records, strength balance fixes, plateau detection, equipment-matched weights, and the weekly coaching report
    • Another program mix and refreshed exercises, unlimited coach circuits, and merging records from two devices
    • Lock Screen Live Activities, plus Home Screen widgets and customization
    • No limits on routines, custom exercises, clients, or equipment
    • No ads: every ad is turned off while PRO is active.

    PRO+

    Everything in PRO, plus Coach tab programs and coaching matched to how you feel that day.

    • Block periodization, meet attempts, and deload suggestions
    • Daily intensity adjusted for sleep, soreness, stress, and HRV, and back-off weights adjusted from your top set
    • Weekly set targets per muscle, and nutrition targets that follow your weight trend
    • Pre-filled warm-up sets, alternative exercise picks, and the weekly hybrid plan
    • Readiness, recovery, and coach program widgets

    Both PRO and PRO+ are monthly or yearly subscriptions. Tap the FitBoost PRO banner at the top of Menu to see prices and a comparison.

    Restoring purchases

    Tap the FitBoost PRO banner in Menu, then Restore purchases at the top right.

    You need to be signed in to the Apple or Google account you bought with. Subscriptions are tied to the store account, so a subscription bought on iPhone won't restore on Android, and the other way around.

    Canceling

    Payments go through your Apple or Google account, so you cancel there.

    • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
    • Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

    With PRO+ active, the top right of the FitBoost PRO screen changes to Manage subscription to take you there. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before renewal, it may renew automatically. Ask the store you paid through for refunds.

    Trying PRO with an ad

    When an ad is available, a Try PRO by watching an ad card appears on the FitBoost PRO screen.

    Watch a short ad to the end and PRO features unlock for a set time. If you don't finish it, nothing unlocks.
