User Guide

Getting started

What to do after installing, your training goal and main focus, and how the five tabs are laid out.

    On this page

    1. When you first open the app
    2. Training goal
    3. Main focus
    4. No account needed
    5. How the app is laid out

    When you first open the app

    Swipe through three intro screens, then answer one page each for your details, your main focus and experience, and your training goal, and you're ready to log. A main focus that includes strength has one more page.

    1. Swipe through the three intro screens and tap Get started on the last one.
    2. On the Tell us about you page, enter your nickname, sex, birth year, height, and weight in one go. For birth year, height, and weight, tap the row to choose.
    3. At What do you mostly train?, pick Mostly strength, Strength and running, or Mostly running, then choose how much training you've done further down the same page (running experience for Mostly running). If your main focus includes running and you've run before, you can also enter a recent running result below that, or leave it blank. Main focus only sets what shows first; every strength and running feature stays available, and you can change it later in Main focus.
    4. Pick your training goal. Goals closest to your main focus come first, and above the list you see the defaults for the goal you picked (Program goal, Program cardio, and Diet). Mostly running ends here.
    5. A main focus that includes strength has one more page. If you chose Just starting, pick where you train and how many times a week (and Not available here if it's a gym), and you get a first routine that fits your goal. If you've trained before, enter your estimated squat 1RM, or tap Not sure or Estimate from a lift if you don't know it.

    On every question page, the Back button at the top left takes you back to the previous page to change it, and what you picked stays as it was. Tap Get started on the last page to finish. Your details are used to suggest weights and estimate calories burned. The weight unit (kg or lb) follows your device's region, and you can change it in settings.

    Training goal

    Your training goal sets the starting choices in the program builder, coach, and nutrition, and the set plan suggestion.

    1. Tap the This week row at the bottom of Today, then the training goal row at the top of the screen that opens, or go to Me → App settings → Training goal.
    2. Pick Strength, Hypertrophy, Fat loss, Recomposition, Muscular endurance, Strength & endurance, or Fitness & health.

    A new goal applies from your next workout. The workout in progress and routines you made stay as they are. If you haven't picked a goal, the one guessed from your records is marked Guessed from your records.

    Main focus

    Main focus only decides whether strength or running shows first. It never hides a feature.

    1. Open Me → App settings → Main focus. It's at the top of settings, under your training goal.
    2. Pick Mostly strength, Strength and running, or Mostly running.
    • Today: on days with no plan, what shows at the top follows your main focus (running for Mostly running, strength for the others). The order of the groups on the This week screen and which ones start folded change too. Mostly strength folds the running group, Mostly running folds the strength group, and Strength and running keeps both open.
    • Plan: the order of strength and running under What goes into your week changes. The first time you open Record → Analysis, your main focus's section opens (Strength for Strength and running); after that, the last section you viewed opens.
    • Me: the order of strength tools, running tools and calculators changes.
    • It sets the starting focus when you first make a weekly plan.

    Main focus is separate from your training goal. The goal is what you want (muscle, fat loss and so on); the main focus is what you do. The strength and running modes of 1.9.0 and earlier are gone. The first time you open 1.9.1, your main focus is set once from the mode you used, and Today shows Strength and running now work together without modes. Change what comes first under Settings > Main focus. once.

    No account needed

    There's no sign-up or login. Your records are stored on this device.

    Change your nickname in Me → Account → Edit profile. Since records aren't sent to our servers, turn on sync across devices or make a backup file so you don't lose them with your phone.

    How the app is laid out

    The tabs at the bottom are Today, Plan, Race, Record, and Me. In 2.0.0 they replaced Home, Record, Stats, Coach, and Menu: stats moved to the Analysis view of Record, the coach, programs, and routines moved to Plan, and the menu moved to Me.

    • Today: the one thing to do today is shown large at the top. A strength day shows the muscle recovery map, a run day the workout structure strip, and a race practice day the segment line, and the button below starts it. Right under that, five shortcuts (Strength, Running, Circuit, Race practice, and Quick record) start a different workout straight away. Tap the This week row at the bottom to open a screen with the Strength, Running, and Body groups. On a rest day you see Rest today and the recovery map, and the shortcuts stay as they are.
    • Plan: what to do each day this week, and What goes into your week. Open Race prep, Strength coach, Programs, My routines, Movement assessment, Coach circuit, Running coach, Form analysis, and Pace zones from here. See The Plan tab and the coach screen.
    • Race: pick a hybrid race format or make your own, practice with every segment timed, and see your race result analysis. See Race prep and practice.
    • Record: pick the Log or Analysis view at the top. Log is the calendar and the list of past workouts, filtered with the All workouts, Strength only, and Runs only chips. Each day on the calendar gets a bar in the sport's color (peach for strength, sky blue for running, the theme color for races), and a taller bar means a longer session. Analysis is the former Stats, where you pick the Strength, Running, or Body section. See What's in the Analysis view.
    • Me: at the top, a strip that stacks each week's workouts this year in sport colors, and the FitBoost PRO row; below them, strength and running tools, health app connections, Transfer & backup, Send to trainer, and Calculators. App settings is the gear button at the top right.

    Your main focus sets the order of the groups on the This week screen and which ones start folded. Tap a group's title to fold or open it; the app remembers, and a folded row still shows this week's numbers. While a workout or run is in progress, the top of Today is the way back to it, and the shortcuts for anything other than what you're doing are dimmed and tell you why when you tap them. The first time you open the app after updating to 2.0.0, Today shows The tabs are now Today, Plan, Race, Record and Me. Stats are under Record › Analysis, and Coach is in Plan. once. On iPad and an unfolded iPhone Duo, the app has two panes: a menu on the left with live numbers on each item, and on the right the recovery map at first, then whatever you pick in the menu.
