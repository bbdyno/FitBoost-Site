    How the Coach tab is laid out

    The shared area at the top of Coach has This week's hybrid plan, the interference note and Hybrid race. Below it, pick the Strength, Running, or Body & nutrition section.

    • This week's hybrid plan PRO+: what to do each day this week (coach session, routine, circuit or run) and which days are done or missed. Without PRO+ it's the locked Plan your hybrid week row. See Weekly hybrid plan.
    • Running alongside lifting: appears when you've done both strength and running recently. See Prescriptions that know your strength training.
    • Hybrid race: opens Hybrid race prep.
    • Strength: block programs, deloads and the readiness check. Only the movement assessment row is free; the rest is PRO+.
    • Running: the running coach. Free.
    • Body & nutrition: cardio fitness (free), plus nutrition targets, body composition coaching and stress and recovery (PRO+).

    The first time, your main focus's section opens (Strength for Strength and running); after that, the last section you viewed opens. The sections always sit in the same order: Strength, Running, Body & nutrition.

    Next-weight suggestions during a workout PRO

    Finish a set and log your effort, and the app tells you what to lift on the next set and when to stop for the day.

    Turn suggestions on or off in Menu → App settings → Workout & routines → In-workout coaching. Free users see a one-time preview per workout. PRO+ also adjusts back-off sets on the spot from your top-set effort and fills in warm-up sets for you.

    Suggestions are a training aid, not medical advice. If something hurts, go lighter and log it with This hurts.

    Coach circuits

    Pick your time, equipment, and intensity, and the app builds a circuit for today. Free gets 3 a week; PRO has no limit.

    1. Tap Home → Programs → Coach circuit, or ⋯ → Get a circuit from your coach on today's card.
    2. The first time, choose time, equipment, and intensity and tap Build circuit. After that, the preview opens with your last choices.
    3. In the preview, use Rebuild, Save as routine, or Circuit settings (voice guidance and sounds), then tap Start.

    When you finish, it asks how it felt and adjusts the next circuit. You can also start one from your watch.

    Weekly hybrid plan PRO+

    It builds a week that mixes strength, circuits, and running. There's one plan, and when you change it, today's card on Home and This week's hybrid plan at the top of Coach update right away.

    1. Tap Home → Programs → Weekly hybrid plan. You can also open it from This week's hybrid plan at the top of Coach, or from the Hybrid plan shortcut in the Running group on Home.
    2. Choose days, goal, focus, cardio, equipment, and age, then tap Build plan. The focus starts from your main focus.
    3. Today's workout shows in the top card on Home. On the plan screen, start a day with its Start button.

    To change the settings, tap Rebuild plan. Routines you edited yourself or put on your schedule are kept, not overwritten. A missed day stays missed and isn't moved to another day. The week after you miss a strength or circuit day, the plan's routines repeat last time's weight and reps instead of going up. If you have a block program, its coach sessions go on the plan's strength days in order, and if you're doing a cardio fitness block, its sessions go on the cardio days.

    Choosing Running focus as the focus gives a plan with more running days, and swaps a quality run for an easy run the day after a leg workout. See the running coach and Prescriptions that know your strength training.

    Block programs in the Coach tab PRO+

    From your goal, meet date, and current 1RMs, it builds a multi-week program and sets the weights for every session.

    1. Tap Coach → Strength → Build program.
    2. Choose your goal (powerlifting, strength, powerbuilding, or hypertrophy), days per week, experience, progression style, body weight, and meet date (optional).
    3. Enter your current 1RMs. If you don't know them, calculate from a weight and reps.
    4. Tap Build program. Today's session appears in the Coach tab and on Home.

    Block programs in the Strength section are PRO+ only. Without PRO+ you'll see the movement assessment row, an introduction, and See PRO+. If you have a weekly hybrid plan, make one from the Run strength days on a coach program row and its coach sessions go on the plan's strength days in order.

    Readiness check PRO+

    Log sleep, muscle soreness, stress, and energy before training, and it adjusts that day's weights and sets.

    Tap Check in on the Readiness card in the Strength or Running section of Coach, fill it in, and tap Save and apply. With a program running, you can also open it from Readiness on Home. Connect a health app or Garmin Connect to include sleep duration, HRV, and resting heart rate.

    Deloads and meet attempts PRO+

    It reads your performance and fatigue trends and suggests a deload or a block extension in the Coach tab.

    It recommends a deload when recent performance drops, sessions feel harder than prescribed, readiness stays low, or six weeks have passed since your last deload. When the program reaches the weeks right before a meet (peaking and taper), it also shows first, second, and third attempts.

    Nutrition targets PRO+

    It reads your body composition or body-weight trend and resets your calorie, carb, protein, and fat targets every week.

    On the Nutrition card in the Body & nutrition section of Coach, enter weight, height, age, activity, and goal, then tap Set targets. With body composition (lean mass) from the last 30 days, maintenance calories and protein come from lean mass instead of body weight, and entering Target body fat shows a goal weight (see Using body composition). The weight trend appears after at least 4 weigh-ins over more than 2 weeks. For a one-off calculation, use the macro calculator.

    Hybrid race prep

    Practice a hybrid fitness race, eight rounds of a 1 km run followed by one station, as if it were the real thing, with a time for every segment. The free plan allows 3 a week.

    1. Open Home → Programs → Hybrid race prep. You can also open it from the Hybrid race row at the top of Coach.
    2. Pick a Division (weights) under Singles, Doubles, or Relay (4), then the practice range (full, half, stations only, or run after stations) and Goal finish time (full race), and each segment's distance or reps, weight, and target appear. For doubles and relay, see Team plan.
    3. Tap Start race practice and press the big button each time you finish a segment. You can also finish it on your watch.
    4. When you're done, you'll see the split table and the segments where you lost the most time. Save with Save and finish.

    Pick a goal from the preset times, or enter hours, minutes and seconds with Custom (30 minutes to 5 hours). Your goal resets the segment targets and the run pace, station total and transition time. If the goal is more than 2% faster than your fastest full practice or the estimate from your week-one diagnosis, the app warns it may be too much. Segment estimates follow studies measured at real races and coaching methods, and parts with no research are marked as field estimates. See the sources under How it is calculated.

    Pick a race date and the remaining weeks are split into base, build, peak, and taper with what to do now, and a week-one diagnosis finds your weakest station. After the race, enter your official time to compare it with your goal and practice. This is a practice tool not tied to any particular event; distances, reps and weights follow the public rulebook.

    A practice started on your phone can be tracked on Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch or a Garmin watch, showing the current segment, its distance or reps and target, the time on that segment, and wrist heart rate. To end a segment, tap Lap on Apple Watch and Galaxy Watch, or press the start button on Garmin. Run segment distance is measured with your phone's location.

    Doubles and relay divisions and the team plan

    Pick a Doubles or Relay (4) division and the key rules appear, and the Team plan card uses your teammates' diagnoses to decide who does what and how much.

    1. In Hybrid race prep, tap Doubles or Relay (4) and pick a division (women, men, mixed and so on).
    2. On the Team plan card, tap a teammate and fill in Teammate tests (1 km run, row, farmers carry, wall balls). Enter your own diagnosis on the race prep card above after setting a race date.
    3. See the team estimate, each station's split, and the pace to run together or the legs each person takes.
    • Doubles: you run every 1 km together, at the pace of whoever is slower alone. Station shares are set so both work for the same time, which means the faster person does more, with switch units such as 250 m on the ergs or 12.5 m on the sled. Mixed doubles both use the men's open weights.
    • Relay: each of the four takes a 1 km run and the station after it twice, and legs are assigned to each person's strong stations to give the lowest total. Mixed relay needs two women and two men, each using their own sex's weights.

    Teammates with no diagnosis are calculated from typical results. In doubles practice, each segment name carries both people's shares; in relay, the runner's name. These go to your watch as well. Switch units with no research behind them (such as 10 wall balls) are marked as field estimates.
