Sharing with a trainer

Send your records to a trainer, and view client records as a trainer.

    On this page

    1. Sending to your trainer
    2. Viewing client records (for trainers)

    Sending to your trainer

    Pick a period, and the app makes a PDF report and a client file to send through any app, like a messenger or email. It's free.

    1. Open Menu → Send to trainer. It's also at the top of Record.
    2. Choose the name for the report, the period (weeks), and what to include (workouts, body records, readiness, notes).
    3. Tap Create and send report and pick the app to send with.

    Files don't go through our servers. Subscriptions, settings, and device details aren't included.

    Viewing client records (for trainers)

    Open a client's file in FitBoost and it's filed by client in Menu → Client records.

    Tap the file you received to open it in FitBoost, or use Open client file on the Client records screen. Client records never mix with your own records or stats.

    Free lets you view 1 client; PRO has no limit. When an existing client sends a new file, it updates even on free. Deleting client records only removes the copy on this device.
