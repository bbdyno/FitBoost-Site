Troubleshooting

What to check when watches, sync, backups, or subscriptions get stuck.

    When your watch won't connect

    Use the message under Watch status in Menu → App settings → Smartwatch to find the cause.

    • No paired watch. Check that the phone and watch are paired in the watch's companion app (Watch app or Galaxy Wearable).
    • Install FitBoost on your watch. Install FitBoost on your Apple Watch or Galaxy Watch.
    • Waiting for your watch Open FitBoost on the watch once.
    • Watches aren't supported on this device. This phone can't use a watch.

    Also check that Smartwatch sync is on. To send a workout to the watch, start it on your phone first.

    When your Garmin won't connect

    Follow the message that appears when you tap Connect a Garmin watch.

    • Install the Garmin Connect app first. Install Garmin Connect on your phone and pair your watch.
    • Choose your watch in the Garmin Connect app. When Garmin Connect opens, choose the watch you'll use with FitBoost.
    • Install FitBoost on your watch. Tap to open the Connect IQ Store.

    If you open FitBoost on the watch and no workout arrives, check that you started the workout on your phone first. The full steps are in connecting a Garmin watch.

    When sync isn't working

    Check the message in Menu → App settings → Sync across devices.

    • You're not signed in to iCloud. Sign in to your Apple Account in the Settings app
    • Google Drive access is needed. Tap Sync now to allow it again
    • Your iCloud storage is full / Your Google Account storage is full Free up or add storage.
    • Another device saved with a newer version of the app. Please update the app
    • Can't connect right now. Will try again shortly

    Check that both devices use the same iCloud or Google account and both have sync on. iPhone and Android don't sync with each other; move with a backup file.

    When a backup file won't open

    A failed import leaves the records on this device unchanged. Check the message you see.

    • This isn't a FitBoost backup file. Make sure it's a .fitboost file.
    • This file was made with a newer version of the app. Update the app, then import it.
    • The file is damaged, so nothing was imported. Your records on this device are unchanged. Try getting the file again.
    • Incorrect password. Enter the password set when the file was made. A forgotten password can't be recovered.
    • This isn't a client file. Import your own backups from Transfer & backup.

    When PRO doesn't show after subscribing

    Tap Restore purchases on the FitBoost PRO screen.

    You need to be signed in to the Apple or Google account you paid with. Subscriptions bought on iPhone don't restore on Android, and the other way around. See restoring purchases for more.

    When a workout you just did is missing

    Workouts ended with End without saving aren't saved. Records are kept only when you tap Finish workout.

    You can re-enter a lost workout with Record → Add workout record → Log a past workout. If you did it on a Garmin watch, keep your phone close and send it again from the watch app.

    When it says a workout is in progress

    If you see Finish the workout in progress first., a workout is still open.

    Go back with Resume workout on today's card in Home and tap Finish workout, or discard it with End without saving if you don't need it, then start again.

    When the health app won't connect

    If you see Couldn't connect health data., check your device's health permissions.

    On iPhone, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → FitBoost. On Android, check FitBoost under app permissions in Health Connect. Nothing new to import. means there are no new workouts from the last 30 days.

    When notifications or the Lock Screen don't show

    If you see Notifications are turned off. Turn them on in Settings., allow notifications for FitBoost in your device settings.

    If Live Activities are off, the app says Turn on Live Activities in Settings › FitBoost. Notifications must be allowed too. See also widgets and the Lock Screen.
