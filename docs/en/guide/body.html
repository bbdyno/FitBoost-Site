Body composition and cardio fitness

Body composition from the health app, per-device trends, using it for diet and goals, and measuring and raising VO2 max.

    On this page

    1. Body composition trend
    2. Body composition from the health app
    3. Using body composition for diet and goals PRO+
    4. Cardio fitness (VO2 max)
    5. Training to raise cardio fitness PRO

    Body composition trend

    The Body tab of Record draws fat mass and lean mass as 7-day average lines, separately for each device. You can also open it from the Body section of Stats and the Body group on Home.

    1. In Record → Body, log weight and body fat with Add body record, or get them from the health app.
    2. After 4 or more measurements on the same device over more than 2 weeks, the Body composition trend card shows fat mass and lean mass trends.
    3. See the formulas and sources under How it is calculated at the end of the card.

    Body composition devices use different formulas, so lines only connect measurements from the same device. Days when lean mass changed a lot overnight are dashed, with a note that it's more likely water or food than muscle. It also shows how much of the weight lost or gained was fat, and the height-adjusted fat-free mass index (FFMI). For readings you can compare, measure in the morning on an empty stomach, after using the toilet, before training, at the same time and on the same device.

    Body composition from the health app

    Weight, height, body fat and lean mass that InBody or scale apps saved to the health app (Apple Health or Health Connect) come in as body records with the source app's name. Values you enter in the app are saved to the health app too.

    1. Connect the health app.
    2. Tap Get weight and body composition in Apple Health (HealthKit) or Health Connect in Menu, or Get body composition from Health in Record → Body. With the connection on, values also come in when you open the Body tab.

    iPhone also reads BMI and waist, and Android body water. The first read covers the last three months; values from the same source at the same time become one record, and reading again doesn't create duplicates. Records you delete don't come back. Weight, body fat and lean mass you enter in the app are written to the health app at that time, unless another app already has a value for that time. If no other app has saved a height to the health app, your profile height is written; if the health app's height is newer, your profile height is updated to it.

    Using body composition for diet and goals PRO+

    With body composition records, nutrition targets are calculated from lean mass instead of body weight, and the Body composition coaching card in the Body & nutrition section of Coach reads the trend and tells you what to change.

    1. Pick the Body & nutrition section in Coach and set nutrition targets on the Nutrition card.
    2. To set a target body fat, enter it in Target body fat in the nutrition setup. It shows a goal weight assuming you keep your lean mass.
    3. If lean mass drops while cutting, the Body composition coaching card shows a warning and what to do; tap Slow the cut to slow the rate of loss and raise calories.

    With lean mass from the last 30 days, maintenance calories come from lean mass, and protein while cutting is 2.3 g per kg of lean mass (3.1 g if lean mass is dropping). When fat mass falls and lean mass rises, it's treated as a recomp and calories aren't cut; when more than 40% of the weight gained while bulking is fat, the surplus is reduced. The upper limit on the rate of loss depends on your body fat. The sources, with research values and coaching-practice standards marked separately, are in Nutrition and body composition sources. Without body composition, it calculates from body weight as before.

    Cardio fitness (VO2 max)

    The Cardio fitness screen gathers VO2 max from the health app, Garmin, field tests and the lab, shows each source separately, and compares you with people of the same age and sex. Measuring and viewing are free.

    1. Open Cardio fitness in the Running group of Menu. You can also open it from the Body section of Stats, the Body & nutrition section of Coach, and the Body group on Home.
    2. If there's no value yet, measure with Cooper 12-minute run, tap Read from the Health app, or enter a lab value with Enter a lab result.
    3. Check the top value, where you stand among people of the same age and sex, and Trend by source.
    • Read from the Health app: reads values that devices such as Apple Watch or Galaxy Watch saved to the health app. The health app connection in settings must be on.
    • Garmin: turn on health summaries in Garmin Connect linking and your running and cycling VO2 max and Garmin fitness age come in.
    • Cooper 12-minute run: warm up, then run as far as you can in 12 minutes. 5 km time trial resets the performance index for your pace zones and isn't converted to VO2 max.
    • Enter a lab result: enter a value measured with breath gas analysis (treadmill or bike) and the date.
    • Max heart rate: a measured value replaces the age estimate. With max heart rate, resting heart rate and sex, an estimate from the heart rate ratio also appears.

    Sources measure differently, so values are never mixed. Follow one source's trend, and retest the same way to see change. The top value is picked in the order lab, field test, device, app estimate, and values older than 180 days aren't used. Each source shows the error from its validation studies, and with your age (20 to 79) and sex in your profile, a treadmill reference table gives your percentile for your age and sex and an approximate fitness age. Values measured on a bike aren't compared with this table. See the sources under See the evidence.

    Training to raise cardio fitness PRO

    Do your next VO2 max session from Training to raise it on the Cardio fitness screen, and with a 6–8 week block, measure first, train, then measure again the same way to compare.

    1. In Training to raise it, check the next session and why it was chosen, then tap Start session. Pick your phone or watch on the run start screen and go.
    2. For a block, choose the length (6 or 8 weeks) and sessions per week in 6–8 week block and tap Start block. Measure a starting value first with the Cooper 12-minute run or a lab value.
    3. When the block ends, measure again the same way you started and compare the start and end values.

    Sessions are chosen from 4-minute repeats, 30 s/15 s and 30 s/30 s based on your last ratings, and afterwards the time above 90% of max heart rate and how well you held the pace decide whether the next one goes up, stays or comes down. On days when HRV is below your usual or readiness is low, an easy run is suggested when you start. Block sessions go on the cardio days of your weekly hybrid plan, and if the value hasn't moved in 4 weeks, the app checks how your intensity is split and tells you. Sessions can be tracked on Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch or a Garmin watch, or sent through Garmin Connect. On the free plan you can measure and view; sessions and blocks show as locked rows.
