User Guide

Health apps

Send workouts to Apple Health and Health Connect, and bring in sleep and heart rate.

    On this page

    1. Connecting a health app
    2. What's shared
    3. Importing watch workouts
    4. Merging duplicate workouts

    Connecting a health app

    iPhone connects to Apple Health, and Android to Health Connect. It's free.

    1. In Menu, open Apple Health (HealthKit) or Health Connect.
    2. Turn on Apple Health & Health Connect and allow what to share in the permission screen that appears.

    The same screen also has Import watch workouts and Merge duplicate workouts.

    What's shared

    Finished workouts go to the health app, and watch workouts, heart rate, sleep, and body weight come back in.

    • Sent: finished strength and cardio workouts. Strength only is saved as strength training; mixed with cardio, as cross training.
    • Read: workouts and heart rate from your watch and health app, plus sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and body weight for readiness and recovery.
    • Runs: the route (location and elevation), distance, and heart rate are written to the health app with the run. Values the health app has no place for are kept in a backup copy on the device, which goes only into backup files and isn't synced.

    On Android, step permission is requested because steps are read along with workouts.

    Importing watch workouts

    Brings workouts from the last 30 days in your health app into your FitBoost records.

    Tap Import watch workouts on the health app screen. Workouts FitBoost wrote are not imported again.

    Merging duplicate workouts

    When several records overlap at the same time, Merge duplicate workouts keeps just one.

    It keeps the one logged in FitBoost, or the one with the most detail, and deletes the rest. Deleted records aren't imported again.
