Movement assessment

Measure mobility, fitness, jumps, bar speed and running form with your phone camera, read the results, apply them to your plan and retest.

    Opening movement assessment

    Open the test list from Movement assessment in Coach in strength mode, or Form analysis in Coach in running mode.

    1. In Coach, tap Movement assessment (Form analysis in running mode).
    2. Pick a test from the Mobility, Fitness, Power and readiness, Barbell or Running groups.
    3. Read how to place the phone, how to do it and when to stop on the setup screen, then start.

    Strength mode shows the shoulder, squat, sit-and-reach, knee extension, straight leg raise, knee-to-wall, push-up, single-leg stance, 30-second chair stand, Countermovement jump, Drop jump and Bar speed and path tests. Running mode shows Running form, side (cadence and landing), Running form, back (pelvis and knees) and the leg mobility, fitness and jump tests. Measuring and viewing results are free.

    How to measure

    Stand the phone up and get into position as the setup screen shows. Once the camera check passes, it counts 3, 2, 1 and guides you by voice.

    1. Place the phone as the setup screen says (each test has its own side, front or angled view).
    2. Tap Measure with camera and stand so your whole body is in view. It starts on its own when the check passes.
    3. When you finish, check the measured value and tap See result.

    Tests the camera doesn't measure, such as sit-and-reach and standing toe touch, take a value you measure with a ruler. If the camera's value is off, tap Edit on the result; edited records are marked Edited · unverified count. Turn on Keep a video of the test to save the measurement as a video on this device only.

    Reading the result

    The result shows the value with its measurement error, change since last time, left–right difference and research reference ranges.

    • A change is called a change only when it's beyond the minimal detectable change (the smallest difference that is more than measurement error).
    • Reference ranges compare you with study samples of the same age and sex. Where evidence is thin, the result tracks only your own change.
    • If you note symptoms such as pain while measuring, a red flag appears above the numbers and plan updates are blocked.
    • Tap Why we read it this way for the method, limits, sources and evidence grade.

    Use Make a result card to turn a result into a share card. The app doesn't diagnose anything. If you have pain, stop exercising and talk to a professional.

    Applying to your plan PRO

    Tap Apply to my plan on the result to see what will change first, then tap Apply to add it to your warm-ups and programs.

    1. In Your plan on the result, tap Apply to my plan.
    2. Review Here's what changes and tap Apply.
    3. When you start a workout, add the warm-up with Add in the line at the top of the list.

    You can undo it any time with Undo. Strength plans change only in strength mode; for running, the cadence target does the job. Anyone can read the suggestions for free.

    Jumps and leg readiness

    Countermovement jump and Drop jump are measured from 240 fps slow-motion video. You check the takeoff and landing frames the app found, then see the result.

    1. On the setup screen, film in the app or pick a slow-motion video from Photos.
    2. Check the frames the app found. If they're off, move one frame at a time with One frame back and One frame forward.

    Measure countermovement jumps a few times and Leg readiness compares today with your usual spread. PRO+ suggests one step lighter lower-body work and quality runs on low-readiness days. Videos that aren't 240 fps aren't accepted.

    Bar speed and path

    During a barbell exercise, tap Measure bar speed in the exercise's ⋯ menu, or open Bar speed and path from the test list.

    1. Choose the plate diameter (450 mm for competition and bumper plates) and stand the phone beside the plate.
    2. Tap Start and do your set.
    3. At Follow this plate?, tap Yes if it's right, or tap the plate's top and bottom edges.

    You get mean propulsive velocity per rep and Velocity loss, shown as an estimate not yet compared with lab equipment. Estimating %1RM from speed is PRO, and a cue to end the set when loss passes your threshold is PRO+. It appears in strength mode only.

    Running form and cadence target

    In running mode, open Form analysis in Coach, pick Running form, side (cadence and landing) or Running form, back (pelvis and knees), and film yourself running on a treadmill or track.

    1. Stand the phone about 1.5 m high and film from the side (or behind).
    2. See cadence, landing position, shin angle, foot strike and more in the result.
    3. Tap Use as target in Cadence target and your phone and watch cue you on easy runs when you leave the range.

    The cadence target is 5–10% above your current cadence at the same speed. Adding it to run guidance is PRO, and PRO+ adds form drills to your weekly running plan. Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch and Garmin show the target range too.

    Retesting and videos

    Each test gets a next date from the evidence interval (4 weeks when there isn't one), with a reminder that morning.

    • The test list and result show the next measurement date. With a PRO+ coach plan, the date lines up with a deload week (running form doesn't).
    • PRO Replay video: overlay the joints and slow down to 0.25×. Side by side with first test: watch alongside your first test, synced to the same moment.
    • Delete videos with Delete this video on the result or in Test video storage. The numbers stay.

    Trainer assessments of members

    Trainers measure members with Assess this member on the member's records screen and send a document with records and assessments using Create report.

    A member's age and sex are used only to choose reference values and stay on this device. Photos and videos are kept only when The member agreed to photos and video is on, and reports include only face-blurred photos. Running form and bar speed aren't available for members. See also Sharing with a trainer.

    Where videos and records go

    Video and joint positions are analyzed on your device and never sent to a server.

    Measured numbers go into backup files and device sync like your other workout records. Measurement videos and face-blurred scene photos stay on this device only and are left out of backups and sync. See the privacy policy for details.
