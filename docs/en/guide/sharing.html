Sharing and customizing

Turn runs and strength workouts into share cards, polish fonts, colors, and photos in the editor, and save your own templates.

    On this page

    2. Opening the editor
    3. Fonts, colors, and photos

    Choosing a share card

    Tap Share run on a run result, pick a Route, Splits, On a photo, or Sticker card, and make it in story or feed size.

    1. On the run result screen, tap Share run. For past runs, open them from Record.
    2. Pick a card from the chips at the top, then choose Story 9:16 or Feed 4:5 and a background color.
    3. Turn items such as Show route, Show average heart rate, and Show ascent on or off.
    4. Tap Share to Instagram Story to open your Instagram story right away. To send it elsewhere or save it to Photos, use Share elsewhere.

    The Sticker card has a transparent background, so it goes into your story as a sticker you can move. The route is drawn only from the line with the start and finish areas and your privacy zones cut out, and cards carry no base map, coordinates, or clock time (the date is included). For strength workouts, make cards the same way from Share workout summary on a record opened in Record, or from Share my stats in Stats.

    Opening the editor

    Tap the magic wand button (Customize) at the top of the run or strength share screen to open the editor.

    1. Tap the magic wand button at the top right of the share screen.
    2. Open Stickers, Fonts, Colors, Photo, Layers, or My templates from the bar at the bottom.
    3. Add stickers and drag them with your finger, or change their size and rotation. Use Add text to add text.
    4. Under Size, choose Story 9:16, Feed 4:5, or Square, then export or post to your story.

    Run stickers include distance, time and pace, the elevation profile, and your route. Strength stickers include Today's workout, Personal record, Big three total, Volume up from last time, Sets, Muscles worked, and more. Stickers with analysis values, such as grade-adjusted pace or the estimated 1RM trend, follow the plan that value belongs to. The app name on cards is always included, whatever your plan.

    Fonts, colors, and photos

    Even within one sticker, pick a font and color separately for Number, Unit, and Label. Photos can be adjusted from grayscale and blur to brightness and contrast.

    • Fonts: a font per part, Size, Spacing, and Fixed-width numbers. If a font lacks some characters, the app tells you Characters this font lacks use the default font.
    • Colors: three basic colors, colors picked from your photo, Eyedropper, and Color code. It shows the contrast so you know the text is readable, and suggests Add shadow.
    • Photo: Black and white, Blur, Brightness, Contrast, Warmth, and Darken bottom.
    • Route: Solid, or color the line by Pace or Grade.
    • Layers: Undo and Redo, Bring forward and Send backward, Hide, Lock, position and rotation values, and Show story safe zones.

    There are 22 fonts, all included in the app under the SIL Open Font License (OFL). Free use gets the three basic fonts for the whole sticker, the three basic colors, grayscale and blur, and a single-color route. A font per part and the other fonts, photo colors, the eyedropper and color codes, undo, layers, values and the safe area, route coloring, and brightness, contrast, warmth, and bottom shade are PRO.

    Putting stickers behind people PRO

    Turn on Place behind person in Photo, and the person in your photo is cut out so stickers go behind them. Photos are processed only on your device.

    1. Pick a photo as the background in the On a photo card or in the editor.
    2. In Photo, turn on Place behind person.

    The person is cut out on this device only, so stickers sit behind them. iPhone uses the Vision feature built into the device, and Android uses a person segmentation model included in the app, so your photo isn't sent anywhere. If the person is small or blurry, you'll see No person found in the photo.

    My templates and auto-create

    Save your layout, fonts, and colors with Save this layout as a template, and they fill in with your next workout's values (PRO).

    1. When your layout is done in the editor, tap Save this layout as a template in My templates.
    2. Next time you share, pick that template in My templates and the new values go in.
    3. Turn on the Auto-made when you finish switch on a template, and after a workout or run it opens straight from Share with my template on the result screen (PRO+).

    Templates are stored only on this device and in backup files, and aren't uploaded by sync across devices. If your subscription ends, saved templates aren't deleted; only the PRO features lock again.

    Free and paid in sharing

    Share cards and basic customization are free. The pro editing tools are PRO, and auto-creating cards from your template when you finish is PRO+.

    • Free: run and strength share cards, adding, dragging, resizing, and rotating stickers, three basic fonts, three basic colors, grayscale and blur, a single-color route, story, feed, and square sizes, and exporting and posting straight to Instagram stories.
    • PRO: font, size, letter spacing, and number width per part, the other fonts, colors from your photo, the eyedropper and color codes, undo, layers, values, and the story safe area, route coloring, brightness, contrast, warmth, and bottom shade, putting stickers behind people, and my templates.
    • PRO+: share cards made from your template right when a workout or run ends.

    The app name on cards is included on every plan, and there's no option to remove it.
