Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

FitBoost is a workout log app. This policy explains what information the app handles and where that information is kept.

Summary

  • You can use the app without an account. We never ask you to sign up.
  • Your workout and body records are stored on your device. If you turn on sync across devices, they are also stored in your own iCloud or Google Drive, where we cannot see them.
  • The standard setup has no server. We do not send your records to our servers.
  • We do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • We collect usage statistics to see how the app is used. The contents of your workout records and advertising identifiers are not sent.

Information the app handles

Workout and body records

This covers workout sessions, sets, weights, reps, routines, schedules, notes, body weight and body composition, and progress photos. This information is written only to your device's storage, and the app does not send it anywhere.

If you have device backup turned on, this information may be included in your iCloud Backup or Google automatic backup. Apple and Google handle those backups under their own policies, and we have no access to them.

Sync across devices (optional)

If you turn on sync across devices in settings, a copy of your records is kept in your own storage so that your devices signed in to the same account stay in step.

  • iPhone and iPad: your iCloud private database (CloudKit)
  • Android: a hidden, app-only folder in your Google Drive. When you turn it on, we ask only for access to that folder in your Google account.

Apple and Google keep this copy under their own policies. It does not pass through our servers, and we have no access to it. Photo and video files and your subscription status are not transferred. Sync is off by default.

Send to trainer (optional)

The app creates a report (PDF) and a client file from the records in a period you choose, and sends them through an app you pick (a messenger, email and so on). The files go only to the person you send them to and do not pass through our servers. The receiving app stores those records only on the recipient's device.

Backup file (optional)

When you create a backup file in Transfer & backup in the menu, the app puts your workouts, routines, schedules, body records, photos and settings into a single file (.fitboost) and hands it to the place you pick (the Files app, a messenger, email and so on). The file also records when it was made, the app version and the device type (iOS or Android). It does not pass through our servers, and if you set a password it cannot be opened without that password. Your subscription status, health app connections and notification permission are not included.

Health data (optional)

Only if you allow it, the app exchanges workout records with Apple Health or Health Connect. If you decline, or turn it off later, the rest of the app keeps working as before. Health data never leaves your device.

Photos and camera (optional)

The app accesses your photo library or camera only when you add a progress photo. The photos you choose are stored on your device, and we do not view them.

Notifications (optional)

Rest timer and workout schedule notifications are generated directly on your device. We do not use a push server.

Usage statistics

To understand which features are used and to improve the app, we collect usage statistics with Google's Firebase Analytics. We collect the following.

  • App usage events: app launches, screen views, workout start and completion, exercise selection, use of weight recommendations, opening the purchase screen and subscription purchases
  • Values and categories attached to events: the number of exercises in a workout, workout duration (minutes), total number of workouts, the catalog number of the selected exercise, and the subscription product and plan type
  • Device and app information: country, device type, operating system and app version
  • App instance ID: a random identifier created anew each time the app is installed

We do not collect the following.

  • The contents of your records, such as weights, reps, notes, body weight and body composition, and photos
  • Your profile, such as nickname, height and body weight
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, AAID) and the iOS identifier for vendors (IDFV)

Usage statistics are not linked to your name or contact details, and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. The statistics are processed on Google's servers.

Ads

The current release has no ads. It ships with the ad feature turned off, so no ad requests are sent and no advertising identifier is read.

If we add ads, we will first describe in this document what information goes to the ad provider, and ask for your consent to personalized ads in the app.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed through your Apple or Google account. We neither receive nor store your card number or any other payment method details.

If a server is connected

Features such as social features, live trainer connections and an AI coach depend on other users, so they need a server. Trainer sharing by file and sync across devices work without a server. The current release does not include these server features. If we offer them in the future, we will update this policy to describe what information is sent to the server, explain it in the app in advance, and ask for your consent.

Retention and deletion

Your records are kept for as long as they remain on your device. When you delete the app, the data it created is deleted with it. You can delete individual records in the app at any time.

Copies included in device backups must be managed in Apple's or Google's backup settings.

Turning off sync across devices does not delete the copies left in iCloud and Google Drive. You can delete FitBoost's hidden data on iPhone in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage, and on Android under Manage apps in Google Drive settings.

Usage statistics are deleted after a set period according to Google Analytics retention settings. When you delete the app, its app instance ID is no longer used, and a new ID is created if you reinstall.

Children

FitBoost is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because your workout and body records are on your device, you can view, correct and delete them yourself in the app. In Transfer & backup in the menu, you can put all of your records into a single backup file to keep wherever you like or move to another device.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its last-updated date.
