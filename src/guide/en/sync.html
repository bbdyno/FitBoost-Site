Sync across devices

Keeps records in step automatically between devices on the same account: iCloud on iPhone and iPad, Google Drive on Android. It's free.

  1. Open {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > cloudSyncTitle}}.
  2. Turn sync on. Tap {{> cloudSyncNow}} to sync right away.
  3. Turn it on the same way on your other device with the same account.

Your records never pass through our servers; they're stored only in your own iCloud or Google Drive.

What doesn't sync

Photo and video files, subscriptions, and workouts in progress stay on each device.

  • Photo and video files aren't copied. Note text is.
  • Subscriptions, workouts in progress, health app and watch connections, and your selected equipment stay per device.
  • Devices on different systems, like iPhone and Android, don't sync automatically. Move with a backup file.

Moving to a new phone

Between phones on the same system, use sync or a device backup. Between iPhone and Android, use a backup file.

  1. On your old phone, tap {{> tabMenu > backupTitle > backupExportTitle}}.
  2. Send the file to your new phone by messenger, email, or cloud storage.
  3. On the new phone, open it with {{> tabMenu > backupTitle > backupImportTitle}}.
  4. Choose {{> backupModeReplace}}.

On the same system, just turning on sync across devices is enough. If device backup (iCloud Backup or Google's automatic backup) is on, your records also come along when you restore the app on a new device. Subscriptions don't move with the file; see restoring purchases.

Creating a backup file

{{> tabMenu > backupTitle > backupExportTitle}} puts all your records into one file. It's free.

It includes workouts, routines, schedules, body records, photos, and settings. You can add a password, but a forgotten password means the file can't be opened, so write it down.

Importing a backup file

Pick a .fitboost file in {{> tabMenu > backupTitle > backupImportTitle}}, then choose replace or merge.

  • {{> backupModeReplace}} (free): clears this device's records and replaces them with the file. Your previous records are backed up automatically so you can undo.
  • {{> backupModeMerge}} (PRO): adds the file to your current records. When both have the same record, the one edited last wins, and records deleted on one side come back.

Keeping your records safe

Records live on this device, so deleting the app deletes them too.

  • Turn on sync across devices or device backup.
  • Make a backup file now and then and keep it somewhere else.
