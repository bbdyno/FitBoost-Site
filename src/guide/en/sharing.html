Choosing a share card

Tap {{> runShareOpen}} on a run result, pick a {{> runShareKindRoute}}, {{> runShareKindSplits}}, {{> runShareKindPhoto}}, or {{> runShareKindSticker}} card, and make it in story or feed size.

  1. On the run result screen, tap {{> runShareOpen}}. For past runs, open them from {{> tabRecord}}.
  2. Pick a card from the chips at the top, then choose {{> shareFormatStory}} or {{> shareFormatFeed}} and a background color.
  3. Turn items such as {{> runShareShowRoute}}, {{> runShareShowHeartRate}}, and {{> runShareShowAscent}} on or off.
  4. Tap {{> shareToInstagramStory}} to open your Instagram story right away. To send it elsewhere or save it to Photos, use {{> shareOtherApps}}.

The {{> runShareKindSticker}} card has a transparent background, so it goes into your story as a sticker you can move. The route is drawn only from the line with the start and finish areas and your privacy zones cut out, and cards carry no base map, coordinates, or clock time (the date is included). For strength workouts, make cards the same way from {{> shareWorkoutSummary}} on a record opened in {{> tabRecord}}, or from {{> shareCardsTooltip}} in {{> tabStats}}.

Opening the editor

Tap the magic wand button ({{> studioOpen}}) at the top of the run or strength share screen to open the editor. The bar at the bottom starts with {{> studioPanelTemplates}}.

  1. Tap the magic wand button at the top right of the share screen.
  2. Open {{> studioPanelTemplates}}, {{> studioPanelStickers}}, {{> studioPanelFonts}}, {{> studioPanelColors}}, {{> studioPanelPhoto}}, or {{> studioPanelLayers}} from the bar at the bottom.
  3. Drag stickers with your finger to move them, and use two fingers to resize or rotate them.
  4. Under {{> studioCanvasSize}}, choose {{> shareFormatStory}}, {{> shareFormatFeed}}, or {{> studioSquare}}, then save to Photos or post to your story.

The first time you open it, a tip shows once explaining how to pick a layout, drag, and press and hold. The panel you opened last opens again next time. On the inner screen of a foldable iPhone or an iPad held sideways, the canvas sits on the left and the tools on the right.

Ready-made layouts

Tap a ready-made layout in {{> studioPanelTemplates}}, the first panel of the editor, and an arrangement filled with this record's values goes in right away. It's free.

  1. Open the editor and open the {{> studioPanelTemplates}} panel.
  2. In the {{" studioPresets}} row, look over the previews filled with this record's values and tap one.
  3. Drag stickers to fine-tune it or add more. You can tap another layout to change just the arrangement.

There are 17 ready-made layouts for runs and 9 for strength. Tapping one changes only the arrangement, and the canvas size, background, and photo stay as they are. Layouts built around a value the record doesn't have are hidden, such as {{> studioPresetBody}} for a run without heart rate. Below them, {{> studioMyTemplates}} holds the templates you saved. See My templates and auto-create.

Kinds of stickers

The {{> studioPanelStickers}} panel is split into {{> studioSectionRecord}}, {{> studioSectionText}}, {{> studioSectionWords}}, {{> studioDecoration}}, and {{> studioSectionPhoto}} groups.

  • {{> studioSectionRecord}}: for runs, {{> studioMetricRunSummary}}, {{> studioMetricElevation}}, and {{> studioRoute}}, plus cadence, {{> studioMetricStartTime}}, {{> studioMetricWeekDistance}}, {{> studioMetricMonthDistance}}, {{> studioMetricMonth}}, and {{> studioMetricRunStreak}}. For strength, {{> studioMetricLiftSummary}}, {{> studioMetricLiftRecord}}, {{> studioMetricBigThree}}, {{> studioMetricVolumeVsLast}}, {{> studioMetricSetChips}}, {{> studioMetricMuscles}}, {{> studioMetricDate}}, {{> studioMetricStartTime}}, and more.
  • {{> studioSectionText}}: {{> studioAddText}}, {{> studioBubble}}, and {{> studioPin}}. After selecting a bubble, use the {{> studioBubbleTail}} button to turn its tail to one of four directions.
  • {{> studioSectionWords}}: word chips such as {{> studioWordLongRun}}, {{> studioWordNegativeSplit}}, and {{> studioWordLegDay}}. There are 18 for runs and 10 for strength.
  • {{> studioDecoration}}: 28 shapes. After adding one, pick its color or switch it to {{> studioOutline}}.
  • {{> studioSectionPhoto}}: {{> studioPhotoSticker}} (square, or round with the {{> studioCircle}} button) and {{> studioPhotoGrid}}.

Stickers with analysis values, such as grade-adjusted pace or {{> studioMetricOneRepMaxTrend}}, follow the plan that value belongs to, and the other stickers are free. The app name on cards is always included, whatever your plan.

Press-and-hold menu and selecting several

Press and hold a sticker, or select it and tap ⋯ ({{> studioMore}}) in the bar below, to open its menu.

  • Menu: {{> actionEdit}} (stickers with text), {{> studioDuplicate}}, {{> studioBringFront}}, {{> studioSendBack}}, {{> studioShadow}}, {{> studioLayerLock}} PRO, {{> studioMultiSelect}}, and {{> actionDelete}}.
  1. Press and hold a sticker and choose {{> studioMultiSelect}}.
  2. Each tap on another sticker adds or removes it. Drag any selected sticker and they all move together.
  3. Use {{> studioSelectAll}}, {{> studioCenterAlign}}, and {{> actionDelete}} in the tool bar to handle them at once, then tap {{> actionDone}}.

The menu except locking, and selecting several, are free. The app name can't be duplicated or deleted.

Saving to Photos

Tap the bottom-left button ({{> studioSaveToPhotos}}) in the editor to save your card to Photos at its current size. It's free.

  1. When you're done, tap the bottom-left button in the editor.
  2. The first time you save, the app asks for photo access. On iPhone it asks only for permission to add photos.
  3. When you see {{" studioSaved}}, you're done.

On Android, cards are saved to the FitBoost album. If you didn't allow access, you'll see {{" studioSaveFailed}} Turn on photo access in your device settings. To send the card to another app, use {{> shareOtherApps}} in the same row.

Fonts, colors, and photos

Even within one sticker, pick a font and color separately for {{> studioPartValue}}, {{> studioPartUnit}}, and {{> studioPartLabel}}. Photos can be adjusted from grayscale and blur to brightness and contrast.

  • {{> studioPanelFonts}}: a font per part, {{> studioFontSize}}, {{> studioLetterSpacing}}, and {{> studioTabular}}. If a font lacks some characters, the app tells you {{" studioFontFallback}}.
  • {{> studioPanelColors}}: three basic colors, colors picked from your photo, {{> studioEyedropper}}, and {{> studioHex}}. It shows the contrast so you know the text is readable, and suggests {{> studioAddShadow}}.
  • {{> studioPanelPhoto}}: {{> studioGrayscale}}, {{> studioBlur}}, {{> studioBrightness}}, {{> studioContrast}}, {{> studioWarmth}}, and {{> studioBottomShade}}.
  • {{> studioRoute}}: {{> studioRouteSolid}}, or color the line by {{> studioRoutePace}} or {{> studioRouteGrade}}.
  • {{> studioPanelLayers}}: {{> actionUndo}} and {{> studioRedo}}, {{> studioLayerUp}} and {{> studioLayerDown}} (free), {{> studioLayerHide}}, {{> studioLayerLock}}, position and rotation values, and {{> studioSafeArea}}.

There are 22 fonts, all included in the app under the SIL Open Font License (OFL). Free use gets the three basic fonts for the whole sticker, the three basic colors, grayscale and blur, and a single-color route. A font per part and the other 19 fonts, photo colors, the eyedropper and color codes, undo, hiding and locking layers, values and the safe area, route coloring, and brightness, contrast, warmth, and bottom shade are PRO.

Putting stickers behind people PRO

Turn on {{> studioSubjectFront}} in {{> studioPanelPhoto}}, and the person in your photo is cut out so stickers go behind them. Photos are processed only on your device.

  1. Pick a photo as the background in the {{> runShareKindPhoto}} card or in the editor.
  2. In {{> studioPanelPhoto}}, turn on {{> studioSubjectFront}}.

{{" studioSubjectFrontHint}}. iPhone uses the Vision feature built into the device, and Android uses a person segmentation model included in the app, so your photo isn't sent anywhere. If the person is small or blurry, you'll see {{" studioNoPerson}}.

My templates and auto-create

Save your layout, fonts, and colors with {{> studioTemplateSave}}, and they fill in with your next workout's values (PRO).

  1. When your layout is done in the editor, tap {{> studioTemplateSave}} under {{> studioMyTemplates}} at the bottom of the {{> studioPanelTemplates}} panel.
  2. Next time you share, pick that template in {{> studioMyTemplates}} and the new values go in.
  3. Turn on the {{> studioTemplateAutoOn}} switch on a template, and after a workout or run it opens straight from {{> studioOpenAuto}} on the result screen (PRO+).

Templates are stored only on this device and in backup files, and aren't uploaded by sync across devices. If your subscription ends, saved templates aren't deleted; only the PRO features lock again.
