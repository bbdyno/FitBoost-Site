When your watch won't connect

Use the message under {{> setWatchStatus}} in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > setWatch}} to find the cause.

  • {{" watchNotPaired}} Check that the phone and watch are paired in the watch's companion app (Watch app or Galaxy Wearable).
  • {{" watchAppMissing}} Install FitBoost on your Apple Watch or Galaxy Watch.
  • {{" watchWaiting}} Open FitBoost on the watch once.
  • {{" watchUnsupported}} This phone can't use a watch.

Also check that {{> setWatchLink}} is on. To send a workout to the watch, start it on your phone first.

When your Garmin won't connect

Follow the message that appears when you tap {{> setGarminConnect}}.

  • {{" setGarminNeedsApp}} Install Garmin Connect on your phone and pair your watch.
  • {{" setGarminChoose}} When Garmin Connect opens, choose the watch you'll use with FitBoost.
  • {{" setGarminInstallWatchApp}}

If you open FitBoost on the watch and no workout arrives, check that you started the workout on your phone first. The full steps are in connecting a Garmin watch.

When sync isn't working

Check the message in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > cloudSyncTitle}}.

  • {{" cloudSyncFailICloudSignedOut}}
  • {{" cloudSyncFailDrivePermission}}
  • {{" cloudSyncFailQuotaICloud}} / {{" cloudSyncFailQuotaDrive}} Free up or add storage.
  • {{" cloudSyncFailUpdateRequired}}
  • {{" cloudSyncFailNetwork}}

Check that both devices use the same iCloud or Google account and both have sync on. iPhone and Android don't sync with each other; move with a backup file.

When a backup file won't open

A failed import leaves the records on this device unchanged. Check the message you see.

  • {{" backupErrorNotBackup}} Make sure it's a .fitboost file.
  • {{" backupErrorNewer}}
  • {{" backupErrorCorrupt}} Try getting the file again.
  • {{" backupWrongPassword}} Enter the password set when the file was made. A forgotten password can't be recovered.
  • {{" memberImportNotMember}}

When PRO doesn't show after subscribing

Tap {{> restorePurchases}} on the FitBoost PRO screen.

You need to be signed in to the Apple or Google account you paid with. Subscriptions bought on iPhone don't restore on Android, and the other way around. See restoring purchases for more.

When a workout you just did is missing

Workouts ended with {{> workoutFinish}} aren't saved. Records are kept only when you tap {{> finishWorkout}}.

You can re-enter a lost workout with {{> tabRecord > histAddWorkout > recordPastWorkout}}. If you did it on a Garmin watch, keep your phone close and send it again from the watch app.

When it says a workout is in progress

If you see {{" finishWorkoutProgressFirst}}, a workout is still open.

Go back with {{> homeContinueWorkout}} on today's card in {{> tabHome}} and tap {{> finishWorkout}}, or discard it with {{> workoutFinish}} if you don't need it, then start again.

The same reason is behind {{" modeLockedActive}} when you switch to running mode.

When the health app won't connect

If you see {{" setHealthLinkFailed}}, check your device's health permissions.

On iPhone, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → FitBoost. On Android, check FitBoost under app permissions in Health Connect. {{" setWatchImportEmpty}} means there are no new workouts from the last 30 days.

When notifications or the Lock Screen don't show

If you see {{" setNotificationPermissionDenied}}, allow notifications for FitBoost in your device settings.

If Live Activities are off, the app says {{" surfaceTurnOnInSettings}}. Notifications must be allowed too. See also widgets and the Lock Screen.

When your run location isn't picked up

If you see {{" runLocationNeeded}} or {{" runGpsDenied}}, turn on location for FitBoost in your device settings.

  1. On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → FitBoost and choose While Using the App.
  2. On Android, go to Settings → Apps → FitBoost → Permissions → Location and choose Allow only while using the app.

You don't need Always. Once a run starts, it keeps measuring with the screen off until that run ends. If {{" runGpsSearching}} lasts a while, wait a moment somewhere with open sky. Indoors, such as on a treadmill, distance is estimated from steps instead of location, so on Android allow physical activity too.

When the map doesn't show

In builds where maps aren't available, your route is drawn as a line with no base map, and {{> courseDraw}} is hidden.

If the course screen says {{" courseDrawUnavailable}}, this is why. You can still make courses with {{> courseFromRun}} or {{> courseFromFile}}, and distance, splits, and course comparisons work as usual. If you see {{" runNoTrack}}, no location was saved for that run, either because it was indoors or because location was off.

When a course link won't open

Tapping a course link opens a course page in your browser. Tap the button there to open it in FitBoost.

Without the app, you can get it from the same page. If you see {{" courseLinkInvalid}}, the end of the link may have been cut off by the messenger, so ask for it again or get a file instead. Open a copied link with {{> coursePasteLink}} under ⋯ at the top right of {{> tabMenu > courseTitle}}. The course page decodes the course in the link only inside your browser and sends it nowhere.
