Creating a routine

Tap {{> actionAdd}} in the {{> homeMyRoutines}} section of {{> tabHome}}.

  1. Tap {{> actionAdd}} in the {{> homeMyRoutines}} section of {{> tabHome}}.
  2. Choose {{> createEmptyRoutine}} or {{> importFromPastRecord}}.
  3. Add exercises and sets, then save.

Free lets you make up to 3 routines; PRO has no limit. You can also save changes back to a routine when you finish a workout.

Editing, duplicating, and deleting routines

On the routines screen, tap ⋯ on a routine for {{> routineEdit}}, {{> duplicateRoutine}}, and {{> deleteRoutine}}.

Open the routines screen from ⋯ → {{> chooseRoutine}} on today's card in {{> tabHome}}. It has three tabs: {{> homeMyRoutines}}, {{> popularPublic}}, and {{> scheduledWorkouts}}. Pick from {{> fitboostPicks}} in {{> popularPublic}}.

Deleting a routine also deletes the schedules and reminders you made with it.

Auto progression PRO

Turn on {{> progToggleTitle}} when editing a routine. Exercises where you hit your target reps on every set last time get a heavier weight next time.

For set-by-set weight suggestions during a workout, see in-workout coaching.

Building a program for you

Pick your goal, schedule, and equipment, and the app builds a week of training and saves it as routines.

  1. Tap {{> tabHome > homePrograms > builderEntryTitle}}.
  2. Choose your goal, experience, days per week, session length, and equipment (gym, home dumbbells, or bodyweight).
  3. Set cardio, up to two muscles to prioritize, and any to rest, then tap {{> builderGenerate}}.
  4. Review the preview and save it as routines.

Weights aren't filled in ahead of time; they're set from your records on the day. Free saves within the 3-routine limit, and {{> builderShuffle}} and {{> builderRegenTitle}} are PRO.

Following a ready-made program

Pick a program at the bottom of {{> tabHome > homePrograms}} and tap {{> startProgram}} to put it on your home schedule.

The top of the same screen has entries for {{> builderEntryTitle}}, {{> circuitTitle}}, and {{> hybridTitle}}.

Custom exercises

Create exercises that aren't in the list with {{> homeExerciseList > addCustomExercise}}.

Set how it's logged (weight and reps, time, and so on), the main muscles, and whether it's done one side at a time. Free allows 5; PRO has no limit.

Exercise list and how-to guides

Search 1,690 exercises in {{> tabMenu > homeExerciseList}}, and open one to see how to do it and what to watch out for.

Each exercise has {{> instructions}}, precautions, tips, your records and stats, and {{> alternatives}}. Tap {{> logThisExercise}} to start logging right away. Search by name or filter by muscle and equipment, and keep the ones you do often in favorites.
