Next-weight suggestions during a workout PRO

Finish a set and log your effort, and the app tells you what to lift on the next set and when to stop for the day.

Turn suggestions on or off in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsWorkout > setSessionCoach}}. Free users see a one-time preview per workout. PRO+ also adjusts back-off sets on the spot from your top-set effort and fills in warm-up sets for you.

Suggestions are a training aid, not medical advice. If something hurts, go lighter and log it with {{> painHereAction}}.

Coach circuits

Pick your time, equipment, and intensity, and the app builds a circuit for today. Free gets 3 a week; PRO has no limit.

  1. Tap {{> tabHome > homePrograms > circuitTitle}}, or ⋯ → {{> circuitEntry}} on today's card.
  2. The first time, choose time, equipment, and intensity and tap {{> circuitMake}}. After that, the preview opens with your last choices.
  3. In the preview, use {{> circuitRegenerate}}, {{> circuitSaveRoutine}}, or {{> circuitSettings}} (voice guidance and sounds), then tap {{> circuitStart}}.

When you finish, it asks how it felt and adjusts the next circuit. You can also start one from your watch.

Weekly hybrid plan PRO+

Mixes strength, circuits, and cardio into a weekly schedule built around the days you train and the days you have more time.

  1. Tap {{> tabHome > homePrograms > hybridTitle}}.
  2. Choose your days, goal, focus, cardio, equipment, and age, then tap {{> hybridMake}}.
  3. Today's session shows up in {{> tabHome}}.

Use {{> hybridMove}} to shift a missed day, and undo it if needed.

Block programs in the Coach tab PRO+

From your goal, meet date, and current 1RMs, it builds a multi-week program and sets the weights for every session.

  1. Tap {{> tabCoach > coachCreatePlan}}.
  2. Choose your goal (powerlifting, strength, powerbuilding, or hypertrophy), days per week, experience, progression style, body weight, and meet date (optional).
  3. Enter your current 1RMs. If you don't know them, calculate from a weight and reps.
  4. Tap {{> coachSetupCreate}}. Today's session appears in the Coach tab and on Home.

The Coach tab is PRO+ only. Without PRO+ you'll see an introduction and {{> coachSeePlans}}.

Readiness check PRO+

Log sleep, muscle soreness, stress, and energy before training, and it adjusts that day's weights and sets.

Tap {{> coachReadinessCheck}} on the {{> homeConditionTitle}} card in the Coach tab, fill it in, and tap {{> coachSaveReadiness}}. With a program running, you can also open it from {{> homeConditionTitle}} on Home. Connect a health app to include sleep duration, HRV, and resting heart rate.

Deloads and meet attempts PRO+

It reads your performance and fatigue trends and suggests a deload or a block extension in the Coach tab.

It recommends a deload when recent performance drops, sessions feel harder than prescribed, readiness stays low, or six weeks have passed since your last deload. When the program reaches the weeks right before a meet (peaking and taper), it also shows first, second, and third attempts.

Nutrition targets PRO+

It reads your body-weight trend and resets your calorie, carb, protein, and fat targets every week.

On the {{> coachNutrition}} card in the Coach tab, enter weight, height, age, activity, and goal, then tap {{> nutritionStart}}. The weight trend appears after at least 4 weigh-ins over more than 2 weeks. For a one-off calculation, use the macro calculator.
