Starting a workout

Tap {{> actionStart}} on today's card in {{> tabHome}}. If you have no routine, you start an empty workout and log freely.

  1. Tap {{> actionStart}} on today's card in {{> tabHome}}.
  2. To use a different routine, tap ⋯ on the card and pick {{> chooseRoutine}}.
  3. Add exercises and log your sets.

You can also start a routine straight from {{> tabHome > homeMyRoutines}}, or go to {{> tabRecord > histAddWorkout > workoutStart}}.

Logging sets

Enter the weight and reps, then tap the check on the set row to finish that set.

  • To finish them all at once, tap {{> completeAllSets}}.
  • Tap the set number to open the set details. Choose the set type (warm-up, drop, failure, and more), grip, effort, and a rest time for that set only.
  • To add drop or back-off sets, or plan the remaining sets at once, see Drop sets, back-off sets, and set plans.

Drop sets, back-off sets, and set plans

Tap {{> composeChip}} on an exercise to plan the remaining sets as {{> composeSchemeStraight}}, {{> composeSchemeTopBackoff}}, or {{> composeSchemeDropFinish}}, with a suggested weight and reps for every set.

  1. Tap {{> composeChip}} on the exercise.
  2. Choose the setup, sets, reps, and reps in reserve. The suggested weight for each set updates.
  3. Tap {{> composeApplyRecommended}}, or tap a row to change its weight and reps first.
  • To add just a drop or back-off set, use {{> sessionAddDropSet}} or {{> sessionAddBackoffSet}} in the exercise's ⋯ menu, or {{> sessionAddDropAfterSet}} in the set editor.
  • When you finish the set before a drop, you go straight into the drop without rest, and rest after the last drop. Watches do the same.
  • The setup that opens first follows your training goal. For example, strength opens big lifts with a top set and back-offs, and hypertrophy opens machine, cable, and isolation moves with a drop at the end.
  • Tap {{> composeSourcesTitle}} to see the studies and coaching methods behind the weights.

Logging effort

When you finish a set, {{" effortQuickTitle}} and four chips appear in one row above Done. Pick one to feed your next-weight math, or ignore it to move on without effort.

Choose how you're asked in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsWorkout > effortPromptSetting}}: {{> effortPromptInline}}, {{> effortPromptSheet}} or {{> effortPromptOff}}. If you turn it off, your watch stops asking too. To log numbers, tap the set and enter RIR or RPE.

Rest timer

The {{> workoutRestTimer}} starts when you finish a set. Add or remove 10 seconds, pause, or skip it.

Set the default rest, alert sound, and whether it keeps running in the background from {{> restTimerOpenSettings}} inside the timer, or in settings.

Exercise menu

Tap ⋯ next to an exercise name to open options for that exercise only.

  • {{> swapExercise}}: replace it with another exercise for the same muscles.
  • {{> setUpSuperset}}: pair it with another exercise and alternate.
  • {{> painHereAction}}: log where it hurts, and exercises that use that area will suggest a lighter weight or a swap. Available on every plan.
  • {{> exerciseUnitToLb}}: log just this exercise in pounds.
  • {{> notes}} and {{> removeExercise}} are here too.

Finishing a workout

Your workout is saved only when you tap {{> finishWorkout}} at the bottom.

  1. Tap {{> finishWorkout}}.
  2. If some sets aren't checked, choose {{> finishUncheckedComplete}} or {{> finishUncheckedSkip}}.
  3. If you started from a routine, choose whether to save your changes to the routine.
  4. On the summary, review volume, time, and muscles used, and add a rating and {{> workoutNotes}}.

{{> workoutFinish}} in the top menu discards the workout. If you see {{" endThisWorkout}} {{" currentRecordWonTBeSaved}}, cancel and tap {{> finishWorkout}} instead.

Logging a past workout

Use {{> homeQuickRecord}} in {{> tabHome}} or {{> tabRecord > histAddWorkout > recordPastWorkout}} to enter a workout with its date.

{{> homeAddScheduled}} in the same menu puts a future workout on your schedule. {{> homeQuickRecord}} won't open while a workout is in progress, so finish that one first.

For cardio, changing the time or distance fills in estimated calories right away, no calculator needed. Calories you typed or imported from a health app stay as they are, and without a body weight on record the app tells you instead of guessing.

Editing or deleting past workouts

In {{> tabRecord}}, tap ⋯ on a workout to edit or delete it.

  • {{> historyEditWorkout}}: change exercises and sets. Stats and next-weight calculations update right away.
  • {{> historyEditDateNote}}: move the date or change the note.
  • {{> startFromThisRecord}}: do the same workout again today.
  • {{> actionDelete}}: deleted workouts can't be recovered.

Body weight and photos

In the {{> body}} tab of {{> tabRecord}}, use {{> histAddBody}} to log weight, body fat, and skeletal muscle mass.

Progress photos go in the {{> photos}} tab with {{> histAddPhoto}}. Your logged weight is used for strength-to-bodyweight levels, cardio calorie estimates, and nutrition targets. You can also import body composition from a file.

Notes

During a workout, use ⋯ → {{> notes}} on an exercise. Afterwards, use {{> workoutNotes}} on the summary.

For notes you'll keep coming back to, like form cues, open the exercise in {{> homeExerciseList}} and use {{> addExerciseNote}}. You can attach photos or videos and pin notes to the top. See every note in {{> tabMenu > menuAllMemos}}.

Voice coach for lifts

With it on, your next set's weight and reps are read out at 10 seconds left and when rest ends, and new records are called out.

Turn it on in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > setVoiceCoach}}. The first time you rest, a one-row offer also appears above Done. It speaks only while the app is open; otherwise you get a notification. Watches buzz once at 10 seconds.
