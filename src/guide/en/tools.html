Plate calculator

Enter a target weight and it shows which plates go on each side. It's free.

Tap {{> plateChip}} under {{> calculators}} in {{> tabMenu}}. Set your bar and plates in {{> gymTitle}}, and use {{> plateCalcChange}} in the calculator to switch equipment.

1RM and program calculators

Enter a weight and reps (15 or fewer) to estimate your 1RM.

In the {{> l1rmCalculator}}, tap {{> oneRmToProgram}} to continue to the {{> programCalculator}}, which works out set weights for 5×5, 5/3/1, 8/6/3, and GVT from that 1RM.

Cardio, calorie, and macro calculators

Open the cardio, calorie, and macro calculators under {{> calculators}} in {{> tabMenu}}.

  • {{> cardioCalculatorTitle}}: calories burned from treadmill speed and incline or erg pace.
  • {{> calorieCalculator}}: estimates calories burned from activity intensity (MET), time, and body weight.
  • {{> macroCalculator}}: target calories plus carbs, protein, and fat.

Every tool is collected in {{> tabMenu > menuAllTools}}.

My equipment

Enter your bar, plates, dumbbell and kettlebell increments, and machine stack step, and the plate calculator matches that equipment.

Open it from {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsWorkout > gymTitle}}. Free keeps one location; multiple locations and rounding suggested weights to what your equipment can make are PRO.

Training glossary

Look up unfamiliar terms like drop sets and RPE in {{> tabMenu > glTitle}}.

Terms are grouped into sets, intensity, and planning, each with what it is, how to do it, when to use it, and what to watch out for.

Importing body composition

Import body composition results from a CSV or JSON file into your body records.

Choose the file in {{> tabMenu > menuAllTools > importBodyComposition}}.

Missing equipment and today's pick

In {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsWorkout > gymTitle}}, mark items under {{> gymMissingTitle}} and exercises that need them are left out of swaps, the program builder and today's pick.

On days without a schedule, the Home today card shows {{> todayEyebrowPick}}: the routine whose main muscles are most recovered and that you have rested longest, with up to three reasons.
