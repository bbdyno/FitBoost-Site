What's in the Stats tab

{{> tabStats}} shows, from the top, your weekly goal, muscle recovery, strength balance, and volume trends.

  • {{> statsWeekVsLastWeek}}: compares this week's training with last week.
  • {{> volumeProgressTrend}} and {{> statsPeriodVolume}}: how your volume has grown.
  • {{> musclesUsed}}: muscles trained over 7, 30, or 90 days, or all time.
  • {{> deeperAnalysis}}: opens {{> smartWeightGuide}} and {{> weeklyCoachingReport}}.

In running mode, a running section appears at the top. It shows distance run, {{> runCapTitle}}, {{> runZonesTitle}}, and {{> runPredictions}}, and with PRO also {{> runStatsLoadTitle}} combining strength and running load, and {{> runCriticalSpeed}}.

Muscle recovery map

A body map shows how recovered each muscle group is. It's free.

It counts hard sets (4 or fewer reps left), counting a full set for the main muscles and half a set for the muscles that helped. If sleep from your health app is under 6 hours, recovery is slowed. Log sore spots with {{> painRecordAction}}.

Strength balance

Uses the last 8 weeks to show how lifts like the squat, bench press, and deadlift compare with each other.

Estimated 1RMs per lift, levels relative to body weight, and adding fixes for the weaker side to your routine are PRO. Levels need your sex in your profile.

Smart weight guide

Pick an exercise to see what you can lift at each rep count and the weight recommended for training.

Open it from {{> tabStats > deeperAnalysis > smartWeightGuide}}. Free shows standard estimates for 3, 5, 8, and 10 reps; PRO uses a 1–15 rep curve built from your own records to set your next weight.

Weekly coaching report PRO

Sums up your week and shows plateau signals and suggestions for your next sessions.

Open it from {{> tabStats > deeperAnalysis > weeklyCoachingReport}}. Applying its set-count suggestions straight to your routines is PRO+.

Sharing stat cards

Use the {{> shareCardsTooltip}} button in {{> tabStats}} to turn your stats into image cards and send them to other apps.

To share one workout as text, use ⋯ → {{> copySharing}} on it in {{> tabRecord}}.

Run share cards and the editor are covered in Sharing and customizing.
