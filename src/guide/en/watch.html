Connecting Apple Watch

Install FitBoost on the Apple Watch paired with your iPhone, then turn on {{> setWatchLink}} on your phone.

  1. Install FitBoost from the Watch app on your iPhone (or the App Store on the watch).
  2. On your phone, open {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > setWatch}}.
  3. Turn on {{> setWatchLink}} and check {{> setWatchStatus}} to see that it's connected.

The watch shows heart rate and active calories during your workout and saves finished workouts to the Health app.

Connecting Galaxy Watch

Works on Wear OS watches like Galaxy Watch paired with an Android phone.

  1. Find FitBoost in the Play Store on your watch and install it.
  2. On your phone, open {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > setWatch}} and turn on {{> setWatchLink}}.
  3. Check {{> setWatchStatus}} to see that it's connected.

While you train, an ongoing workout notification stays on the watch.

Connecting a Garmin watch

Pair your watch with the Garmin Connect app, install FitBoost on the watch from the Connect IQ Store, then tap {{> setGarminConnect}} once.

  1. Pair your watch with the Garmin Connect app.
  2. Install FitBoost on the watch from the Connect IQ Store.
  3. On your phone, tap {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > setWatch > setGarminConnect}}. On iPhone, pick your watch when Garmin Connect opens, then come back.

Works with both iPhone and Android. Workouts done on the Garmin go into your phone's records and are also saved to Garmin Connect as strength training. Linking your Garmin Connect account to receive runs and health summaries and to send courses and workouts is Garmin Connect linking.

Garmin Connect linking

Link your Garmin account with {{> garminConnectTitle}} and runs measured on your Garmin watch, plus sleep and recovery data, come straight in, and you can send courses and workouts you pick to Garmin. It's free.

  1. Tap {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > setWatch > garminConnectTitle}}.
  2. Read what's received and sent, sign in with Garmin, and turn on the items to share in Garmin's consent screen.
  3. Back in the app, new records come in each time you open it. To get them right away, tap {{> garminConnectSyncNow}}.
  • {{> garminConnectRuns}}: runs with route, heart rate, cadence and power become FitBoost runs.
  • {{> garminConnectHealth}}: sleep duration and stages, overnight HRV and resting heart rate are used for readiness, and running and cycling VO2 max and Garmin fitness age go into cardio fitness.
  • {{> garminConnectCourses}} and {{> garminConnectWorkouts}}: tap {{> garminSendAction}} on a course screen, the run start screen, or a routine's ⋯ menu, and it's saved to your Garmin Connect account and moved to your watch. For workouts, you pick the day on the Garmin Connect calendar.

Each item shows on or off; turn off items back on under connected apps in your Garmin Connect account settings. {{" garminSendWatchNote}} Records from Garmin are deleted from the server as soon as your device downloads them, and after 30 days even if it doesn't. Tap {{> garminConnectDisconnect}} to unlink and delete what's left on the server; runs already received stay in the app.

What you can do on the watch

Finish a set and pick your effort, and the rest timer runs and buzzes when it's done. Workouts finished on the watch go into your phone's records.

  • Load the phone workout: continue a workout you started on your phone.
  • Quick strength start: start right on the watch without your phone.
  • Coach circuit: pick a length and your phone builds a circuit that runs on the watch.
  • Complete or undo sets, pick one of four effort levels, and finish and send the workout to your phone.
  • Running: run with pace ranges, steps, and the course line on your wrist, and send the run to your phone when you finish. See Running with a watch.

On Apple Watch, double-tap the weight and turn the Digital Crown to change it. A Garmin watch saves finished workouts while your phone is out of range and sends them when it's back.

Sending the current workout to your watch

Start the workout on your phone first, then tap {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > setWatch > setSendToWatch}}.

You can also load the phone workout from the watch app. If it won't connect, see troubleshooting.
