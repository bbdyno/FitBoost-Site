When you first open the app

Swipe through three intro screens, then answer one page each for your details, your main focus and experience, and your training goal, and you're ready to log. A main focus that includes strength has one more page.

  1. Swipe through the three intro screens and tap {{> onboardingStart}} on the last one.
  2. On the {{" obProfileTitle}} page, enter your nickname, sex, birth year, height, and weight in one go. For birth year, height, and weight, tap the row to choose.
  3. At {{> obModeTitle}}, pick {{> trainingMixStrength}}, {{> trainingMixHybrid}}, or {{> trainingMixRunning}}, then choose how much training you've done further down the same page (running experience for {{> trainingMixRunning}}). If your main focus includes running and you've run before, you can also enter a recent running result below that, or leave it blank. Main focus only sets what shows first; every strength and running feature stays available, and you can change it later in {{> trainingMixTitle}}.
  4. Pick your training goal. Goals closest to your main focus come first, and above the list you see the defaults for the goal you picked ({{> obGoalFxProgram}}, {{> obGoalFxCardio}}, and {{> obGoalDiet}}). {{> trainingMixRunning}} ends here.
  5. A main focus that includes strength has one more page. If you chose {{> obExpShortFirst}}, pick where you train and how many times a week (and {{> gymMissingTitle}} if it's a gym), and you get a first routine that fits your goal. If you've trained before, enter your estimated squat 1RM, or tap {{> notSure}} or {{> obSquatFromLift}} if you don't know it.

On every question page, the {{> actionBack}} button at the top left takes you back to the previous page to change it, and what you picked stays as it was. Tap {{> onboardingStart}} on the last page to finish. Your details are used to suggest weights and estimate calories burned. The weight unit (kg or lb) follows your device's region, and you can change it in settings.

Training goal

Your training goal sets the starting choices in the program builder, coach, and nutrition, and the set plan suggestion.

  1. Tap the {{> todayWeek}} row at the bottom of {{> tabToday}}, then the training goal row at the top of the screen that opens, or go to {{> tabMe > setAppSettings > trainingGoalTitle}}.
  2. Pick {{> trainingGoalStrength}}, {{> trainingGoalHypertrophy}}, {{> trainingGoalFatLoss}}, {{> trainingGoalRecomp}}, {{> trainingGoalEndurance}}, {{> trainingGoalHybrid}}, or {{> trainingGoalHealth}}.

A new goal applies from your next workout. The workout in progress and routines you made stay as they are. If you haven't picked a goal, the one guessed from your records is marked {{" trainingGoalInferred}}.

Main focus

{{> trainingMixTitle}} only decides whether strength or running shows first. It never hides a feature.

  1. Open {{> tabMe > setAppSettings > trainingMixTitle}}. It's at the top of settings, under your training goal.
  2. Pick {{> trainingMixStrength}}, {{> trainingMixHybrid}}, or {{> trainingMixRunning}}.
  • {{> tabToday}}: on days with no plan, what shows at the top follows your main focus (running for {{> trainingMixRunning}}, strength for the others). The order of the groups on the {{> todayWeek}} screen and which ones start folded change too. {{> trainingMixStrength}} folds the running group, {{> trainingMixRunning}} folds the strength group, and {{> trainingMixHybrid}} keeps both open.
  • {{> tabPlan}}: the order of strength and running under {{> planSourcesTitle}} changes. The first time you open {{> tabRecord > recordsViewAnalysis}}, your main focus's section opens ({{> sectionStrength}} for {{> trainingMixHybrid}}); after that, the last section you viewed opens.
  • {{> tabMe}}: the order of strength tools, running tools and calculators changes.
  • It sets the starting focus when you first make a weekly plan.

Main focus is separate from your training goal. The goal is what you want (muscle, fat loss and so on); the main focus is what you do. The strength and running modes of 1.9.0 and earlier are gone. The first time you open 1.9.1, your main focus is set once from the mode you used, and {{> tabToday}} shows {{" oneAppNotice}} once.

No account needed

There's no sign-up or login. Your records are stored on this device.

Change your nickname in {{> tabMe > menuAccountPill > editProfile}}. Since records aren't sent to our servers, turn on sync across devices or make a backup file so you don't lose them with your phone.

How the app is laid out

The tabs at the bottom are {{> tabToday}}, {{> tabPlan}}, {{> tabRace}}, {{> tabRecord}}, and {{> tabMe}}. In 2.0.0 they replaced Home, Record, Stats, Coach, and Menu: stats moved to the {{> recordsViewAnalysis}} view of {{> tabRecord}}, the coach, programs, and routines moved to {{> tabPlan}}, and the menu moved to {{> tabMe}}.

  • {{> tabToday}}: the one thing to do today is shown large at the top. A strength day shows the muscle recovery map, a run day the workout structure strip, and a race practice day the segment line, and the button below starts it. Right under that, five shortcuts ({{> sectionStrength}}, {{> sectionRunning}}, {{> todayQuickCircuit}}, {{> todayQuickRace}}, and {{> homeQuickRecord}}) start a different workout straight away. Tap the {{> todayWeek}} row at the bottom to open a screen with the {{> sectionStrength}}, {{> sectionRunning}}, and {{> sectionBody}} groups. On a rest day you see {{" todayRestTitle}} and the recovery map, and the shortcuts stay as they are.
  • {{> tabPlan}}: what to do each day this week, and {{> planSourcesTitle}}. Open {{> raceHubTitle}}, {{> planCoachStrength}}, {{> homePrograms}}, {{> homeMyRoutines}}, {{> assessEntryTitle}}, {{> circuitTitle}}, {{> planCoachRunning}}, {{> assessEntryRunTitle}}, and {{> runZonesTitle}} from here. See The Plan tab and the coach screen.
  • {{> tabRace}}: pick a hybrid race format or make your own, practice with every segment timed, and see your race result analysis. See Race prep and practice.
  • {{> tabRecord}}: pick the {{> recordsViewLog}} or {{> recordsViewAnalysis}} view at the top. {{> recordsViewLog}} is the calendar and the list of past workouts, filtered with the {{> historyAllWorkouts}}, {{> historyStrengthOnly}}, and {{> historyRunsOnly}} chips. Each day on the calendar gets a bar in the sport's color (peach for strength, sky blue for running, the theme color for races), and a taller bar means a longer session. {{> recordsViewAnalysis}} is the former Stats, where you pick the {{> sectionStrength}}, {{> sectionRunning}}, or {{> sectionBody}} section. See What's in the Analysis view.
  • {{> tabMe}}: at the top, a strip that stacks each week's workouts this year in sport colors, and the FitBoost PRO row; below them, strength and running tools, health app connections, {{> backupTitle}}, {{> trainerShareTitle}}, and {{> calculators}}. {{> setAppSettings}} is the gear button at the top right.

Your main focus sets the order of the groups on the {{> todayWeek}} screen and which ones start folded. Tap a group's title to fold or open it; the app remembers, and a folded row still shows this week's numbers. While a workout or run is in progress, the top of {{> tabToday}} is the way back to it, and the shortcuts for anything other than what you're doing are dimmed and tell you why when you tap them. The first time you open the app after updating to 2.0.0, {{> tabToday}} shows {{" tabsNotice}} once. On iPad and an unfolded iPhone Duo, the app has two panes: a menu on the left with live numbers on each item, and on the right the recovery map at first, then whatever you pick in the menu.
