When you first open the app

Swipe through three intro screens, fill in five profile details, and you're ready to log.

  1. Swipe through the intro and tap start on the last screen.
  2. Enter a nickname, your sex, birth year, height, and weight.
  3. Enter your estimated squat 1RM. If you don't know it, choose that you're not sure.

Your details are used to suggest weights and estimate calories burned. The weight unit (kg or lb) follows your device's region, and you can change it in settings.

No account needed

There's no sign-up or login. Your records are stored on this device.

Change your nickname in {{> tabMenu > menuAccountPill > editProfile}}. Since records aren't sent to our servers, turn on sync across devices or make a backup file so you don't lose them with your phone.

How the app is laid out

The tabs at the bottom are {{> tabHome}}, {{> tabRecord}}, {{> tabStats}}, {{> tabCoach}}, and {{> tabMenu}}.

  • {{> tabHome}}: today's workout, {{> homeQuickRecord}}, {{> homePrograms}}, and {{> homeMyRoutines}}.
  • {{> tabRecord}}: past workouts by date, where you edit or delete them.
  • {{> tabStats}}: weekly goal, muscle recovery, strength balance, and volume trends.
  • {{> tabCoach}}: PRO+ block programs and readiness coaching. Without PRO+ you'll see an introduction only.
  • {{> tabMenu}}: {{> setAppSettings}}, {{> backupTitle}}, {{> trainerShareTitle}}, calculators, and health app connections.
