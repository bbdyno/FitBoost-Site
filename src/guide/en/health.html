Connecting a health app

iPhone connects to Apple Health, and Android to Health Connect. It's free.

  1. In {{> tabMenu}}, open {{> healthKitTitle}} or {{> healthConnectTitle}}.
  2. Turn on {{> setHealthLink}} and allow what to share in the permission screen that appears.

The same screen also has {{> setWatchImport}}, {{> setHealthBackfill}}, and {{> setHealthMergeDuplicates}}.

What's shared

Finished workouts and body records go to the health app, and watch workouts, heart rate, sleep, body composition and cardio fitness come back in.

  • Sent: finished strength and cardio workouts (strength only as strength training, mixed with cardio as cross training), the weight, body fat and lean mass you enter in the app, and your profile height if no other app has saved a height to the health app.
  • Read: workouts and heart rate from your watch and health app; sleep, HRV and resting heart rate for readiness and recovery; weight, height, body fat and lean mass saved by apps such as InBody or your scale (plus BMI and waist on iPhone, and body water on Android); and VO2 max saved by your watch. See Body composition from the health app and Cardio fitness.
  • Runs: the route (location and elevation), distance, and heart rate are written to the health app with the run. Values the health app has no place for are kept in a backup copy on the device, which goes only into backup files and isn't synced.

On Android, step permission is requested because steps are read along with workouts.

Importing watch workouts

With the health app link on, workouts from the last 30 days in your health app come into your FitBoost records automatically when you open the app.

FitBoost reads them each time you open or return to the app, so you don't need to tap anything. To import right away, tap {{> setWatchImport}} on the health app screen. Automatic reads never show a permission prompt and are skipped if the last read was less than 10 minutes ago. On Android, if a read permission is missing, nothing is read automatically; tap {{> setWatchImport}} and allow the permission it asks for. Workouts FitBoost wrote are not imported again. Older workouts come in only through {{> setHealthBackfill}}, which rereads a full year. Runs come in as runs; see Runs from your health app. Workouts from a Garmin watch can also come in without the health app through Garmin Connect linking; if the same workout arrives both ways, only one is kept.

Runs from your health app

Runs (outdoor and treadmill) that other apps wrote to your health app come in automatically when you open the app and become FitBoost runs.

  1. Turn on workout sharing to the health app in the app you use. On iPhone, open the Garmin Connect app's settings → Connected Apps → Apple Health and turn sharing on (apps such as Strava also write to Apple Health). On Android, turn on Health Connect sharing in apps such as Garmin Connect, Samsung Health, and Strava.
  2. In FitBoost, connect the health app. After that, runs come in automatically when you open or return to the app; to bring them in right away, tap {{> setWatchImport}}. To bring in earlier runs too, tap {{> setHealthBackfill}}.
  3. In {{> tabRecord}}, tap the {{> historyRunsOnly}} chip, open the imported run to see its results, then share and customize it.

Imported runs count in your history, stats, coach, weekly distance, and share studio, and treadmill runs come in as indoor runs. Distance and time come from the health app, and the route and heart rate are read again from the health app when you open the result screen. If the health app has no route, no route is shown. The first connection reads only the last 30 days; {{> setHealthBackfill}} rereads a full year and skips anything already imported. The {{> setHealthGarminTitle}} row on the same screen also shows how to turn sharing on. If runs don't come in automatically, check that sharing to the health app is on in the app you use, then tap {{> setWatchImport}}. On Android, this asks for any missing read permission. Each import always rereads at least the last 7 days, so runs that reached the health app late come in on the next import. Runs that came in as a single cardio line before 1.8.0 are also turned into runs if they fall in that window, keeping any name or note you edited. Other cardio such as cycling stays cardio. Runs measured on a Garmin watch can also come straight in without the health app through Garmin Connect linking, or from a GPX or TCX file.

Merging duplicate workouts

When several records overlap at the same time, {{> setHealthMergeDuplicates}} keeps just one.

It keeps the one logged in FitBoost, or the one with the most detail, and deletes the rest. Deleted records aren't imported again.
