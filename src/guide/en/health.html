Connecting a health app

iPhone connects to Apple Health, and Android to Health Connect. It's free.

  1. In {{> tabMenu}}, open {{> healthKitTitle}} or {{> healthConnectTitle}}.
  2. Turn on {{> setHealthLink}} and allow what to share in the permission screen that appears.

The same screen also has {{> setWatchImport}}, {{> setHealthBackfill}}, and {{> setHealthMergeDuplicates}}.

What's shared

Finished workouts go to the health app, and watch workouts, heart rate, sleep, and body weight come back in.

  • Sent: finished strength and cardio workouts. Strength only is saved as strength training; mixed with cardio, as cross training.
  • Read: workouts and heart rate from your watch and health app, plus sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and body weight for readiness and recovery.
  • Runs: the route (location and elevation), distance, and heart rate are written to the health app with the run. Values the health app has no place for are kept in a backup copy on the device, which goes only into backup files and isn't synced.

On Android, step permission is requested because steps are read along with workouts.

Importing watch workouts

Brings workouts from the last 30 days in your health app into your FitBoost records.

Tap {{> setWatchImport}} on the health app screen. Workouts FitBoost wrote are not imported again. For older workouts, {{> setHealthBackfill}} rereads a full year. Runs come in as runs; see Runs from your health app.

Runs from your health app

Runs (outdoor and treadmill) that other apps wrote to your health app become runs in FitBoost when you import them with {{> setWatchImport}}, and show up in running mode.

  1. Turn on workout sharing to the health app in the app you use. On iPhone, open the Garmin Connect app's settings → Connected Apps → Apple Health and turn sharing on (apps such as Strava also write to Apple Health). On Android, turn on Health Connect sharing in apps such as Garmin Connect, Samsung Health, and Strava.
  2. In FitBoost, connect the health app and tap {{> setWatchImport}}. To bring in earlier runs too, tap {{> setHealthBackfill}}.
  3. Open the imported run from {{> tabRecord}} in running mode to see its results, then share and customize it.

Imported runs count in running mode's history, stats, coach, weekly distance, and share studio, and treadmill runs come in as indoor runs. Distance and time come from the health app, and the route and heart rate are read again from the health app when you open the result screen. If the health app has no route, no route is shown. The first connection reads only the last 30 days; {{> setHealthBackfill}} rereads a full year and skips anything already imported. The {{> setHealthGarminTitle}} row on the same screen also shows how to turn sharing on. Each import always rereads at least the last 7 days, so runs that reached the health app late come in on the next import. Runs that came in as a single cardio line before 1.8.0 are also turned into runs if they fall in that window, keeping any name or note you edited. Other cardio such as cycling stays cardio. There's no direct Garmin Connect account link, so go through the health app or import a GPX or TCX file.

Merging duplicate workouts

When several records overlap at the same time, {{> setHealthMergeDuplicates}} keeps just one.

It keeps the one logged in FitBoost, or the one with the most detail, and deletes the rest. Deleted records aren't imported again.
