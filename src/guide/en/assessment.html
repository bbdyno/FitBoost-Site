Opening movement assessment

In both modes, open the test list from the {{> assessEntryTitle}} row ({{> assessEntryRunTitle}} in running mode) right below the quick-start row on {{> tabHome}}, from {{> tabMenu}}, or from {{> tabCoach}}.

  1. On {{> tabHome}}, tap {{> assessEntryTitle}} ({{> assessEntryRunTitle}} in running mode). The same row is in {{> tabMenu}} and {{> tabCoach}}.
  2. Pick a test from the {{> assessGroupMobility}}, {{> assessGroupFitness}}, {{> assessGroupPower}}, {{> assessGroupBarbell}} or {{> assessGroupRunning}} groups.
  3. Read how to place the phone, how to do it and when to stop on the setup screen, then start.

Tests use plain names, with the expert term (such as shoulder flexion) in small type underneath. Strength mode shows {{> mobilityTestShoulderFlexion}}, {{> mobilityTestShoulderAbduction}}, {{> mobilityTestSquatSide}}, {{> mobilityTestSquatFront}}, {{> mobilityTestSitReach}}, {{> mobilityTestToeTouch}}, {{> mobilityTestKneeExtension}}, {{> mobilityTestLegRaise}}, {{> mobilityTestKneeToWall}}, {{> mobilityTestPushUp}}, {{> mobilityTestStance}}, {{> assessTestChairStand}}, {{> jumpTestCmj}}, {{> jumpTestDrop}}, {{> barSpeedTestName}}. Running mode shows {{> runFormTestSide}}, {{> runFormTestBack}} and the leg mobility, fitness and jump tests. Measuring and viewing results are free.

How to measure

Stand the phone up and get into position as the setup screen shows. Once the camera check passes, it counts 3, 2, 1 and guides you by voice.

  1. Place the phone as the setup screen says (each test has its own side, front or angled view).
  2. Tap {{> assessStartCamera}} and stand so your whole body is in view. It starts on its own when the check passes.
  3. When you finish, check the measured value and tap {{> assessSeeResult}}.

Tests the camera doesn't measure, such as sit-and-reach and standing toe touch, take a value you measure with a ruler. If the camera's value is off, tap {{> assessEdit}} on the result; edited records are marked {{" assessEditedBadge}}. Turn on {{> assessVideoKeep}} to save the measurement as a video on this device only.

To watch the screen while you measure on your own, tap {{> assessCameraFront}} at the top right of the preview. The camera you pick is kept for next time. Many front cameras record fewer frames per second, so the rear camera is more accurate for {{> jumpTestCmj}} and {{> jumpTestDrop}}, and ground contact time may be missing from side-view running form.

Reading the result

The result shows the value with its measurement error, change since last time, left–right difference and research reference ranges.

  • A faceless grey mannequin in the test position, under the value, overlays joint points, measured lines and angles so you can see what was measured. If the left–right difference is bigger than the measurement error, the weaker side is orange and the stronger side green.
  • A change is called a change only when it's beyond the minimal detectable change (the smallest difference that is more than measurement error).
  • Reference ranges compare you with study samples of the same age and sex. Where evidence is thin, the result tracks only your own change.
  • If you note symptoms such as pain while measuring, a red flag appears above the numbers and plan updates are blocked.
  • Tap {{> assessWhyTitle}} for the method, limits, sources and evidence grade.

Use {{> assessCardMake}} to turn a result into a share card. The app doesn't diagnose anything. If you have pain, stop exercising and talk to a professional.

Applying to your plan PRO

Tap {{> assessPlanButton}} on the result to see what will change first, then tap {{> assessPlanApply}} to add it to your warm-ups and programs.

  1. In {{> assessPlanSection}} on the result, tap {{> assessPlanButton}}.
  2. Review {{> assessPlanPreviewTitle}} and tap {{> assessPlanApply}}.
  3. When you start a workout, add the warm-up with {{> assessPlanAdd}} in the line at the top of the list.

You can undo it any time with {{> assessPlanRevert}}. Strength plans change only in strength mode; for running, the cadence target does the job. Anyone can read the suggestions for free.

Jumps and leg readiness

{{> jumpTestCmj}} and {{> jumpTestDrop}} are measured from 240 fps slow-motion video. You check the takeoff and landing frames the app found, then see the result.

  1. On the setup screen, film in the app or pick a slow-motion video from Photos.
  2. Check the frames the app found. If they're off, move one frame at a time with {{> jumpFramePrev}} and {{> jumpFrameNext}}.

Measure countermovement jumps a few times and {{> jumpReadinessTitle}} compares today with your usual spread. PRO+ suggests one step lighter lower-body work and quality runs on low-readiness days. Videos that aren't 240 fps aren't accepted.

Bar speed and path

During a barbell exercise, tap {{> barSpeedMeasureSet}} in the exercise's ⋯ menu, or open {{> barSpeedTestName}} from the test list.

  1. Choose the plate diameter (450 mm for competition and bumper plates) and stand the phone beside the plate.
  2. Tap {{> barSpeedStart}} and do your set.
  3. At {{" barSpeedPlateFound}}, tap {{> barSpeedPlateConfirm}} if it's right, or tap the plate's top and bottom edges.

You get mean propulsive velocity per rep and {{> barSpeedLossTitle}}, shown as an estimate not yet compared with lab equipment. Estimating %1RM from speed is PRO, and a cue to end the set when loss passes your threshold is PRO+. It appears in strength mode only.

Running form and cadence target

In running mode, choose {{> runFormTestSide}} or {{> runFormTestBack}} from {{> assessEntryRunTitle}} on {{> tabHome}}, {{> tabCoach}} or {{> tabMenu}}, then film yourself running on a treadmill or track.

  1. Stand the phone about 1.5 m high and film from the side (or behind).
  2. See cadence, landing position, shin angle, foot strike and more in the result.
  3. Tap {{> runFormTargetOn}} in {{> runFormTargetTitle}} and your phone and watch cue you on easy runs when you leave the range.

The cadence target is 5–10% above your current cadence at the same speed. Adding it to run guidance is PRO, and PRO+ adds form drills to your weekly running plan. Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch and Garmin show the target range too.

Retesting and videos

Each test gets a next date from the evidence interval (4 weeks when there isn't one), with a reminder that morning.

  • The test list and result show the next measurement date. With a PRO+ coach plan, the date lines up with a deload week (running form doesn't).
  • PRO {{> assessVideoReplay}}: overlay the joints and slow down to 0.25×. {{> assessVideoCompare}}: watch alongside your first test, synced to the same moment.
  • Delete videos with {{> assessVideoDelete}} on the result or in {{> assessStorageTitle}}. The numbers stay.

Trainer assessments of members

Trainers measure members with {{> assessMemberMeasure}} on the member's records screen and send a document with records and assessments using {{> assessMemberReport}}.

A member's age and sex are used only to choose reference values and stay on this device. Photos and videos are kept only when {{> assessMemberConsent}} is on, and reports include only face-blurred photos. Running form and bar speed aren't available for members. See also Sharing with a trainer.

Where videos and records go

Video and joint positions are analyzed on your device and never sent to a server.

Measured numbers go into backup files and device sync like your other workout records. Measurement videos and face-blurred scene photos stay on this device only and are left out of backups and sync. See the privacy policy for details.
