Weight and distance units

Choose kilograms or pounds in {{> setWeightUnit}} under {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsGeneral}}.

The plate calculator uses the unit you choose. {{> setLengthUnit}} and {{> setWeekStart}} are on the same screen. To log just one exercise in pounds, tap ⋯ → {{> exerciseUnitToLb}} during the workout.

Language

Choose Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsLanguage}}.

Choose {{> langSystem}} to follow your device language.

Appearance and theme

Choose light, dark, or {{> appearanceSystem}} in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsAppearance}}, and pick an accent color in {{> settingsTheme}}.

Rest timer settings

Set the default rest, alert sound, and timer behavior in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsRestTimer}}.

  • {{> setDefaultRest}}, {{> setRestStep}}, {{> setRestSound}}
  • {{> setBackgroundTimer}}: the timer keeps running after you leave the app and alerts you when it ends.
  • {{> setAdaptiveRest}}: sets your next rest from the effort and type of the set you just did. Rests you set yourself and rests in routines or programs are left alone.

Workout and routine settings

Change how the workout screen behaves in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsWorkout}}.

  • {{> gymTitle}}: enter your bar, plates, and dumbbells.
  • RPE/RIR: choose how you log set effort.
  • {{> setSessionCoach}}: turn next-weight suggestions on or off.
  • {{> setAllowDuplicate}}, {{> setSwipeDeleteSet}}, {{> setAutoAdvance}}

To keep the screen from turning off mid-workout, turn on {{> setKeepAwake}} in {{> settingsGeneral}}. Set how much the weight steps up in {{> setWeightStep}} under {{> settingsKeyboard}}.

Notifications

Turn {{> setNotifyBackgroundTimer}} and {{> setNotifySchedule}} on or off in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > settingsNotification}}.

If notifications don't arrive, check that FitBoost notifications are allowed in your device settings.

Widgets and the Lock Screen

Choose {{> surfaceTabLive}} or {{> surfaceTabWidgets}} in {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings > surfaceSettingsTitle}}.

  • Free includes the today's workout widget. Other widgets, customization, and Lock Screen Live Activities (live notifications on Android) are PRO. Readiness, recovery, and coach program widgets are PRO+.
  • Live Activities need iOS 16.1 or later.
  • iPhone: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit at the top left → Add Widget, and find FitBoost.
  • Android: touch and hold the Home Screen, open Widgets, and find FitBoost.

Deleting all records

{{> settingsDeleteLocalData}} at the bottom of {{> tabMenu > setAppSettings}} erases every record on this device.

This can't be undone. Make a backup file before you delete.
